## 1. The frame

**Why we're raising a B.** Series A ($10M closed Q1 2027) funded us through v1.0 pilot (Q1-Q3 2027), v1.0 GA (Q4 2027), v1.1 mesh OTA (Q3 2027), and v1.2 international launch (Q1 2028) at 5 EU markets + UK. Series A dollar carries us to Y2 revenue of $201M and Y2 EBITDA of $8M (per `SERIES-A-DECK.md` Slide 12) with Q4 2028 as the first cash-flow-positive quarter. Series B is not survival capital — it is v2.0 acceleration capital + Y3 international scale-out (16 markets in `EXPANSION-Y2-PLUS.md` §2) + the CTO-plus-full-bench organizational build the founder-CEO cannot continue to run partially-loaded past 24 months post-A.

**Timing — raise from strength, not runway pressure.** The v0.1 outline targeted Q1 2029 close. That date is arithmetically identical to the base-case cash-out date documented in `FINANCIAL-MODEL-SENSITIVITY.md` §8: base-case operating burn of $555k/mo × 24 months from Q1 2027 = $13.3M consumed against $10M Series A + $3M SVB LOC = $0 cash by end of Q1 2029. If Q4 2028 EBITDA-positive slips by one quarter (which §5 Obj 4 concedes is possible), the raise collides directly with cash-out and Series B becomes bridge-priced. v0.2 fixes this by moving close to **Q3 2028** — 18 months post-A close, with dataroom open Q2 2028 and partner meetings Q3 2028. Rationale: raise off Y1 delivered + Q1-Q2 2028 tracking-to-plan strength, not off promised-Q4-2028-breakeven risk. Series A cash covers us through the raise close with 5-6 months of buffer even in base case. Q4 2028 breakeven prints post-close as a validated forecast the Series B lead already underwrote, not as a pitch dependency.

**Amount and price.** $40M raise at $260M pre-money = $300M post = 13.3% new-issuance dilution, ~15% total with pool refresh. Price rationale: 1.5× Y2 revenue forecast ($201M × 1.5 = $302M), sanity-checked against Sonos IPO 1.4× (Aug 2018, `SERIES-A-DECK.md` Slide 14) and Ring acquisition 6.8× (2018). We are asking for the low end of the hardware+services band, not the Peloton multiple. This preserves optionality: bulls can argue we should be marked higher at close, bears cannot argue we are asking above comp.

**What the money is for.** 40% engineering + international + concierge scaling ($16M) / 25% inventory + working capital ($10M) / 20% marketing + brand ($8M) / 10% G&A ($4M) / 5% contingency ($2M). Detailed in §7. Notably: no bucket funds a pivot; every bucket funds a documented commitment in `PRODUCT-ROADMAP-12-24MO.md` or `EXPANSION-Y2-PLUS.md`.

**One-line frame for the cover slide.** *"$40M for 15% at $260M pre — v2.0 hardware refresh, 16-market international scale, and the concierge economics moat that carries us to IPO."*

## 5. Anticipated Series B objections + planned responses

Nineteen objections growth investors will raise. For each: honest reframe, backing document, and prep to walk out of the room if pushed too hard. v0.2 adds four material Y2-stage concerns (Obj 16-19) missing from v0.1.

1. **"Y1 numbers behind plan by X%."** *Reframe:* if X ≤ 10%, we ship the reforecast with Q3 2028 recovery plan and a Q4 2028 in-line-with-plan trajectory. If X ≤ 20%, the round happens smaller ($30-32M at $180-200M pre — bear case in `FINANCIAL-MODEL-SENSITIVITY.md` §4.3).
