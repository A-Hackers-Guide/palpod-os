## Slide 4 — Product

**Hearth is a 14" matte-black sphere. The face floats. Everything else disappears into your house.**

- **Offline general-purpose AI** — Llama 3.1 70B local, Whisper STT, Piper TTS, no cloud round-trip on core intents
- **Media server** — Plex / Jellyfin / AudioBookshelf / xTeVe, 40TB local NAS, 4K HDR to every room
- **Personality** — animated OLED face (Hearth's original companion character), context-aware, remembers your family
- **Extenders** — $8,999 satellite pucks; every room gets voice, audio, and presence
- **Concierge** — 3 years included in main SKU: install, updates, on-call human, overnight tier funded
- **Design** — Halbach-array magnetic levitation on the OLED face only; compute stack, NAS, GPU, and amps all stationary in base

**SPEAKER NOTES:** One clarification because engineers always ask: only the face levitates. The face is a lightweight OLED disk suspended in a Halbach array — physics is honest, no gyroscope, no fans in the levitating part, no batteries, no wireless coil trying to power 400 watts of compute. The compute stack, the NAS, the GPU, the amps all stay stationary in the base. That's what makes it a shippable appliance and not a research demo. Six custom PCBAs are in relayout with Sanmina Fremont right now under a signed 14-week SOW. That's the reason we can promise Q1 2027 delivery instead of vaporware.
